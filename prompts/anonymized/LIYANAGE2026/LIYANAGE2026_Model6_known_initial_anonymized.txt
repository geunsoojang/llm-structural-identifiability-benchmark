For the following ODE model, determine the structural identifiability of each unknown parameter from the observed output. Do not use tools or code; reason from the model equations only.

ODE system:
x1'(t) = p5 - (p1*x1(t)*x4(t) + p2*x1(t)*x5(t))/c1 - p6*x1(t)
x2'(t) = (p1*x1(t)*x4(t) + p2*x1(t)*x5(t))/c1 - p6*x2(t)
x3'(t) = -p3*x2(t)*x3(t)/c1
x4'(t) = p7*p3*x2(t)*x3(t)/c1 - p4*x4(t)
x5'(t) = (1-p7)*p3*x2(t)*x3(t)/c1 - p4*x5(t)
x6'(t) = p4*x4(t) + p4*x5(t)

State variables:
x1(t), x2(t), x3(t), x4(t), x5(t), x6(t)

Unknown parameters:
p1, p2, p3, p4, p5, p6, p7

Known constants or inputs:
c1 and c2 are known constants, with c2 = x3(0).

Initial conditions:
x1(0), x2(0), x3(0), x4(0), x5(0), x6(0) are known.

Observed output:
y1(t) = p7*(c2 - x3(t))

Assumptions:
The observed output variables are available continuously over a time interval and are noise-free.
All parameters are positive, and initial conditions are nonnegative.
The known initial conditions x1(0), x2(0), x3(0), x4(0), x5(0), x6(0) are positive.
The output y1(t) is a cumulative event count. The aggregate total quantity c1 is constant.

Report:
1. Classify each unknown parameter as globally identifiable, locally identifiable, or non-identifiable.
2. If individual parameters are non-identifiable but identifiable combinations exist, state those combinations.

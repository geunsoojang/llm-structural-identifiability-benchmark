x1(t), x2(t), x3(t), x4(t), x5(t), x6(t)

p1, p2, p3, p4, p5

None

x1(0), x2(0), x3(0), x4(0), x5(0), x6(0) are unknown.

Observed output:
y1(t) = p4*x2(t)
y2(t) = p4*x4(t)

Assumptions:

The model includes two interaction processes with rates p1 and p2.

Report:

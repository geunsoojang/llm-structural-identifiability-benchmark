S'(t) = -alpha*(E(t) + I(t) + A(t))*S(t)/N - sigma*S(t)

I'(t) = beta1*h*E(t) + beta2*r*A(t) - phi*q*I(t) - gamma*(1-q)*I(t)
A'(t) = beta1*(1-h)*E(t) - beta2*r*A(t) - gamma*(1-r)*A(t)
J'(t) = phi*q*I(t) - gamma*J(t)
R'(t) = gamma*(1-q)*I(t) + gamma*(1-r)*A(t) + gamma*J(t)

State variables:
S(t), E(t), I(t), A(t), J(t), R(t)

Unknown parameters:
alpha, sigma, h, r, q, f, beta1, beta2, phi, gamma, I0

Known constants or inputs:
N

Initial conditions:
S(0) = 0.9*N
E(0) = 9*(I0 + A(0))
I(0) = I0
A(0) = I0*f
J(0) = 0
R(0) = 0

Observed output:
y1(t) = I(t)
y2(t) = J(t)

Assumptions:
The observed output variables are available continuously over a time interval and are noise-free.
All unknown parameters are positive and constant.
Known constants are positive.
The initial conditions satisfy the stated parameter-dependent relationships exactly.

Report:
1. Classification of each parameter.
